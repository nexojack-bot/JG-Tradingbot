What this strategy does

Ranks today's implied volatility against the stock's OWN trailing history (not other stocks) — a genuinely different question from term structure or skew. Needs roughly 20 days of accumulated history before it can score anything; expect it to sit idle for its first few weeks of real operation.

Formula

IV Rank = fraction of trailing-history days with IV below today's IV, scored when Rank ≤ 0.20
