What this strategy does

ADX measures trend STRENGTH regardless of direction. This strategy requires both a strong trend (ADX above 25) and a bullish direction (EMA9 above EMA20) — ADX alone can't tell you which way the trend runs.

Formula

+DI = 100 × smoothed(+DM) / ATR; −DI = 100 × smoothed(−DM) / ATR;
DX = 100 × |+DI − −DI| / (+DI + −DI); ADX = smoothed(DX)
+DM / −DM — Directional movement — how much of today's high/low range extends beyond yesterday's, in the up or down direction respectively.
ATR — Average True Range, used to normalize directional movement into a percentage.
