What this strategy does

A volume-weighted version of RSI — momentum that's specifically confirmed by trading volume, not just price. Scores symbols with MFI between 50 and 80.

Formula

Typical Price = (High+Low+Close)/3; Money Flow = Typical Price × Volume;
MFR = sum(positive Money Flow14) / sum(negative Money Flow14); MFI = 100 − 100/(1+MFR)
Money Flow — Dollar volume traded at the day's typical price, signed by whether the typical price rose or fell from the prior day.
