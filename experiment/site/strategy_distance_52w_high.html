What this strategy does

Scores symbols within 3% of their own 52-week high — a momentum-continuation thesis (stocks making new highs tend to keep making them), the opposite read from 'expensive, must fall.'

Formula

distance = (High52w − Close) / High52w; score = 1 − distance, when distance ≤ 0.03
High52w — Highest daily high over the trailing 252 trading days (~1 year).
