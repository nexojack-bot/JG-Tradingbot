What this strategy does

The Hurst exponent classifies whether a price series behaves more like a genuine trend (H>0.5), a random walk (H=0.5), or mean-reverting noise (H<0.5). Scores symbols with H above 0.55 that are also in a short-term uptrend — a statistically trending regime, not just a lucky recent run.

Formula

H = 0.5 × slope of log(std(Pt+τ − Pt)) vs. log(τ), for a range of lags τ
τ — Lag (in days) used to measure how price dispersion grows over time.
H — Hurst exponent — the slope of that log-log relationship, scaled.
