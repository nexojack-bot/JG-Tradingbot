What this strategy does

Scores a stock that opened notably BELOW yesterday's close (a gap down of more than 1.5%), on the classic trading hypothesis that such gaps tend to partially 'fill' back toward the prior close. A daily-bar approximation — a true intraday gap-fill strategy needs minute-level data.

Formula

gap = (Closeyesterday − Opentoday) / Closeyesterday, scored when gap > 0.015
