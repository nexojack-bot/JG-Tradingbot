What this strategy does

Scores a symbol breaking above its own prior 20-day high, sized by how many Average True Ranges (ATR) above that high it's trading — normalizes the breakout size across stocks with very different price levels and volatility.

Formula

score = (Close − High20) / ATR14, when Close > High20
High20 — The highest daily high over the prior 20 days (excluding today).
ATR14 — Average True Range over 14 days — the average of the daily high-low range, adjusted for gaps.
